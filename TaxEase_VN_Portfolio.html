01 — The Problem

A policy shock hitting
millions unprepared

Effective January 1, 2026, Vietnam's lump-sum tax system is abolished. Every household business must now self-declare quarterly — with zero government-provided tools to do it.

📋

Mandatory Self-Declaration

All 3.83M registered household businesses must now track revenue, compute VAT + PIT, maintain 4 accounting books, and file quarterly returns. Previously, tax was a fixed annual lump sum — no records needed.

From Jan 1, 2026
🧾

E-Invoice Mandate

Household businesses with revenue above 1B VND/year must now issue General Department of Taxation-compliant e-invoices for every transaction. Currently, only ~110,000 of 3.83M household businesses issue any e-invoices at all.

110K / 3.83M complying
💸

Steep Penalty Exposure

Late filing (>91 days): 8–15M VND. Failure to issue invoice: 10–20M VND. For a business earning 1B VND/year, one compliance mistake costs 1–2% of annual revenue.

8–20M VND per violation
📊

Zero Accounting Literacy

70%+ of household business owners cannot distinguish the two tax calculation methods. 85% use only Excel or paper ledgers. 45% of first-time filers make errors that trigger re-assessment.

45% error rate, first filers
The trigger moment: From January 2026, Shopee and TikTok Shop automatically withhold tax from seller payouts — creating immediate reconciliation demand for 601,800 e-commerce sellers who suddenly see money missing from their accounts.
02 — The Solution

Tax compliance
for non-accountants

TaxEase is built for zero accounting literacy. Scan a receipt, get your tax liability. No jargon, no manual entry, no accountant needed.

📷

AI Invoice Scanner (OCR)

Camera captures paper bills and receipts. Google Vision + VietOCR auto-populate all 4 mandatory accounting books. >92% accuracy on Vietnamese handwritten text.

🧮

Smart Tax Engine

Deterministic rule-based engine covers all 6 business types, 3 VAT/PIT rate combinations, and all 4 household business groups. Auto-selects optimal method for the user.

🧾

One-Tap E-Invoice

Generates General Department of Taxation-compliant PDF + XML e-invoices per Circular 78/2021. Full integration with General Department of Taxation's national e-invoice schema v2.0.

🔔

Proactive Compliance Alerts

Firebase push + Zalo OA webhook alerts before every quarterly deadline. Real-time penalty risk warnings as revenue approaches thresholds.

💬

Plain-Language UX

Replaces accounting jargon with everyday terms. Visual-first design for users with no digital accounting background.

🟦

Zalo Chatbot Onboarding

Full setup via Vietnam's #1 app (76M active users). Reaches rural and suburban household businesses where CAC via Zalo OA approaches near zero with B2G bundling.

Compliance-as-a-Service modularity: The tax rule engine is decoupled from the UI layer — when new Circular regulations are published, rules update via CI/CD pipeline in <30 days without requiring a full app redeployment.
